Questions

Straight answers

Is AcneGone free?

Yes. Pill log, dose journey, private vault, alignment camera, reminders, trends, share progress, and PDF export are all included. No ads. No subscriptions.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your treatment data stays on your phone.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Progress photos are stored only on this device. They are not uploaded to AcneGone servers and are kept out of your camera roll.

Is it only for Accutane?

No. You can track isotretinoin (Accutane) treatment, or use AcneGone skin-only for private progress photos and symptoms.

Is AcneGone a medical device?

No. AcneGone is a personal tracking tool and daily diary. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your dermatologist or qualified healthcare provider regarding prescription medications like Isotretinoin/Accutane.
