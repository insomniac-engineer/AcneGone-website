Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

What AcneGone is

AcneGone is a personal skin tracker. If your dermatologist prescribed pills, you can also keep a private dose diary. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat acne, and does not replace advice from a qualified clinician.

What stays on your phone

Progress photos, pill logs, symptoms, dosage history, dose goals, and dermatologist visits are stored only on this device. Photos are not uploaded to AcneGone servers. Android backup of app data is disabled so those files are not copied to Google Drive.

Optional anonymous analytics

If you opt in, AcneGone sends anonymous app events (for example that a photo was saved, not the photo itself) plus an app-generated device ID, platform, and app version. We do not send pill status, symptoms, body weight, dose amounts, or spot counts. Approximate country can be inferred from the network request (Cloudflare country header) to understand where the app is used.

The analytics backend is hosted on Supabase. The mobile app uses only the public anon key and write-only server functions — other users’ rows are not readable from the app.

What we do not collect

We do not create accounts, collect your name or email, or use your data for advertising. Photos and treatment details are not sold or shared with other companies for their own use.

Your choices

You can decline analytics on first launch or turn it off later in Profile. Turning it off stops new events and deletes the anonymous analytics rows tied to this device. Reset my progress erases treatment data and photos on the device and also deletes those analytics rows. Uninstalling the app removes local data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the AcneGone GitHub repository.
